NOTE: The OCR service does not have its own separate prompt.

It reuses Translation's exact same pipeline (buildHybridDocxBlob / buildOfflineDocxBlob in js/translation-offline.js), just with the target language locked to "original" (i.e. it extracts and rebuilds the layout, but skips the actual translation step).

The prompts it actually uses at runtime are Translation's:
- translation-1.txt (line-by-line OCR extraction) and/or
- translation-2.txt (region-grounding OCR extraction)

depending on which extraction mode is active. There is nothing to edit here specifically - edit the corresponding translation-*.txt file instead, and the change applies to OCR too.
